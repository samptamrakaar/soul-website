Fun Animated Movie Carrying Deep Message

My thoughts:

Obviously, I loved the movie that’s why I made this website. It’s a fantasy comedy-drama which beautifully animated with a great story. The movie is really fun and made me laugh several times. Moving the comedy aspect aside, what I liked the most about the movie is that it tries to convey a deep meaning. The movie tries to tell that people might get disconnected from life by obsessing too much over something. My perspective towards the world has kinda changed after watching the movie. So, I’d highly recommend everyone to watch it.
